### Confirm the taxpayer type

Read the business registration and determine whether the entity is a corporation, general individual VAT taxpayer, simplified taxpayer, exempt business, or mixed business.

### Build two calendars

Maintain an operational calendar for invoice issuance and monthly close, plus a statutory calendar for preliminary and final VAT compliance. Do not wait for the filing month to organize evidence.

### Reconcile output and input VAT

Match sales ledgers to tax invoices, cards, cash receipts, platforms, and bank deposits. Match purchase claims to valid tax invoices, imports, business use, and payment evidence.

### Review exceptions

Exports, cross-border services, exempt supplies, bad debts, fixed assets, and early refunds can change both documents and timing. Obtain advice before applying zero rating or claiming a large refund.
