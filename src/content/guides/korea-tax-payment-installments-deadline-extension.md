### Identify the exact obligation

Write down the tax authority, tax type, taxable period, filing due date, payment due date, amount, notice number, and whether the balance came from a self-filed return or an assessment. National income tax and local income tax should not be treated as one payment merely because they arise from the same income.

### Keep filing on track

If the return can be completed accurately, file by the deadline even when cash is short. Do not insert false figures to reduce the payment. If the return itself cannot be filed, determine separately whether a filing extension exists and what evidence is required.

### Compare the available payment routes

Check for a statutory installment rule first. If it does not apply or is insufficient, ask the responsible authority about an extension of payment or collection relief based on the actual circumstances. Explain the cause, requested period, proposed payment plan, and available security or supporting evidence when relevant.

### Obtain a verifiable decision

Save the submitted application, receipt number, approval or rejection, revised due dates, virtual accounts, and each payment receipt. Continue monitoring notices and Hometax. If the deadline has already passed, request a current payoff calculation and address the balance promptly rather than assuming a pending inquiry stops additions.
