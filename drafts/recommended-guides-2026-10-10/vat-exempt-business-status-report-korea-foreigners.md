### Confirm your activity before choosing a form

Start with your business registration and actual work. A foreign nationality or an English job title does not identify the correct reporting route. Keep the Korean activity code beside your records when asking the tax office to confirm your position.

| Your situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| VAT-exempt individual business | Whether the business status report and industry attachments apply |
| Zero-rated activity | The applicable VAT return route |
| Simplified VAT taxpayer | The applicable VAT reporting rules |
| Mixed taxable and exempt activities | Whether exempt revenue was already reported through the VAT return |

Article 78 provides a deemed-reporting rule where a mixed business reports its exempt revenue through the applicable VAT return. It also includes businesses that closed or suspended operations during the year. Confirm the facts before submitting a duplicate report or assuming closure removed the obligation.

### Who should check the annual report?

The NTS overview lists exempt individual businesses and specific exclusions, including qualifying tax-association reporting and certain commission activities. An exemption from VAT alone does not establish an exclusion from this report.

The January 2026 NTS notice included medical practices, academies, housing rental businesses and certain personal-service businesses. It also described expanded guidance for creators and delivery or driving service providers. A notification list is not a substitute for checking your own activity.

### Match the income year to the filing year

The statutory deadline is February 10 of the following year. For example, NTS instructed businesses to report **2025 income by February 10, 2026**. That completed cycle is not the deadline for 2026 income.

For 2026 records, prepare for the following year's reporting cycle and confirm the official 2027 calendar, holiday adjustments and any extensions when published. This guide does not present a 2027 campaign deadline as already announced.

### Prepare one reconciled set of records

The 2026 notice describes reporting annual revenue, major business expenses and operational information such as equipment and employees. Hometax and Sontax provide assistance and prefilled information for eligible filers. Compare those figures with your own records.

A practical preparation sequence is:

1. Create a folder for the income year and each business registration.
2. Compare your ledger with bank receipts, platform statements and invoices. Explain differences rather than forcing totals to match.
3. Separate refunds, transfers between your accounts and customer payments so the same movement is not counted twice.
4. Mark unresolved items for your tax representative before entering final totals.
5. Save the submitted form, attachments and acceptance receipt together.

The NTS overview identifies revenue review schedules and invoice summary statements among attachments required for relevant businesses. Check the forms for your industry. It also distinguishes industry-specific non-reporting penalties from statement-submission penalties; do not apply one penalty percentage to every exempt business.

### Submit and preserve the result

Use the current official Hometax or Sontax instructions for 사업장현황신고, or confirm filing arrangements with the tax office responsible for your business. Review the business identifier, income year and attachment list before submission. A saved draft is not evidence of acceptance.

Keep a short reconciliation note explaining any difference between your source records and the submitted totals. If you later find an omission, ask how to correct the report and any affected statements.

### Prepare the income tax return separately

Use the report as a reconciliation checkpoint for the [annual income tax return](/guides/global-income-tax-filing-korea-foreigners/). Keep the same source ledger and explain later adjustments. Reporting revenue here does not by itself settle income tax, establish deductible expenses or complete other tax obligations.
