### What the November payment covers

Income Tax Act Article 65 uses January 1 through June 30 as the interim period and generally bases the assessed payment on half the statutory prior-year income tax base. The ordinary collection date is November 30. This is an advance income tax payment, not a separate tax on foreign nationality.

Read the full notice before acting. Confirm your name, taxpayer identifier, tax name, period, amount and payment reference. Keep an electronic copy together with the envelope or delivery record.

### Three different tax tasks

| Task | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Income tax interim prepayment / 중간예납 | Notice, taxpayer category and first-half reporting rules |
| VAT preliminary notice / 부가가치세 예정고지 | VAT registration and its separate payment instructions |
| Annual global income tax return / 종합소득세 확정신고 | Full-year income, allowable expenses and prepaid tax |

Use the [VAT preliminary notice guide](/guides/korea-vat-preliminary-payment-notice-foreign-business/) if the document is a VAT bill. For annual filing, start with [global income tax filing](/guides/global-income-tax-filing-korea-foreigners/).

### Check whether the rules apply to you

The NTS lists exclusions, including specified income-only situations and qualifying new-business cases. Do not assume every foreign worker owes an interim payment merely because they filed an annual return. Conversely, a business owner should not assume employment withholding resolves business-income obligations.

Bring the notice, prior return and business commencement details to the tax office or your representative when your category is unclear. Check actual taxpayer status rather than using the visa label as the answer.

### When first-half estimated reporting matters

The NTS 2026 guidance allows estimated reporting when calculated first-half income tax is below 30% of the interim base. That is a tax calculation test, not a sales-decline test. It also describes mandatory reporting for certain resident double-entry bookkeeping taxpayers with first-half business activity but no interim base.

The guidance lists November 30, 2026 as the submission and payment deadline. It explains that a below-threshold calculated payment can still require submission to cancel a notice. Confirm the applicable route before simply paying less.

Prepare January-June books, expense evidence and withholding records. Identify missing transactions and ask your representative to check the calculation before choosing this route. For records, see [bookkeeping requirements](/guides/bookkeeping-requirements-korea-foreign-freelancers/).

### A practical action checklist

1. Identify whether the document is income tax, VAT or local tax.
2. Match its period and identity details to your records.
3. Check the payment date and any extension actually granted to you.
4. Review exclusions or estimated reporting before changing the requested payment.
5. Use official payment instructions and save the completed transaction receipt.
6. Keep any submitted return and acceptance record with the calculation papers.

If you need online payment navigation, see [how to pay Korean tax online](/procedures/how-to-pay-korean-tax-online/). Opening a payment page is not proof that the transaction completed.

### Keep the annual reconciliation file

Store the interim notice, payment confirmation and any estimated return together. When preparing the annual return, compare these records with the prepaid tax shown in the calculation. Investigate mismatches instead of entering the same amount twice or assuming it was imported.

This guide was reviewed on October 11, 2026. Recheck official November instructions and your own notice before acting, because later relief or individual extensions may affect the payment date. Complex residence, bookkeeping or classification questions need an individual review.
