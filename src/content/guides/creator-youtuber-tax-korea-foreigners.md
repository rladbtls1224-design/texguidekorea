### Identify the activity before choosing a tax category

The [NTS creator guidance](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=7802&mi=2480) distinguishes media-content businesses with staff or business facilities, code **921505**, from independent creators without those features, code **940306**. The latter category is VAT-exempt; that does not remove income-tax reporting or the separate business-status reporting described by the NTS. The guidance also addresses money received from viewers: the label “donation” does not by itself settle its tax treatment.

Describe your operation in ordinary words before selecting a code: what you produce, how often, who pays, whether you hire staff, and what studio or business equipment you use. Sponsorship sales, consulting, physical products and courses may need their own classification analysis. A channel name is not an accounting category.

### Build a revenue map you can explain

Use one line per revenue stream and keep the original evidence. This is an organizational worksheet, not a declaration that every row has the same tax treatment.

| Revenue stream | Evidence to retain | Reconciliation question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform advertisements or subscriptions | Monthly activity and payout exports | Which month's activity does this transfer settle? |
| Sponsorship or affiliate work | Contract, invoice and settlement statement | Are commissions, returns or deductions shown separately? |
| Viewer support | Platform ledger or bank-transfer record | Is it already included in another settlement? |
| Products or services supplied to a sponsor | Agreement and delivery evidence | What was promised in exchange, and how was it valued? |
| Courses, consulting or product sales | Order records, receipts and refunds | Is this the same activity as the video channel? |

Record the original currency, date, amount and document reference. For foreign-currency items, keep the conversion method and supporting rate record with your tax working papers; have the appropriate method checked for the relevant year and transaction type.

### Fictional example: reconcile a payout without calculating tax

A platform statement shows KRW 1,000,000 of activity, a KRW 100,000 platform charge and a KRW 900,000 bank payout. Record all three figures with their relationship. Do not record the statement and the payout as two independent sales, and do not assume the net deposit alone explains the full transaction.

This example illustrates reconciliation only. It does not establish the legally reportable gross amount, the deductibility of the charge, the recognition date or the VAT result. Those depend on the contract and applicable rules. The point is to preserve enough evidence to answer those questions.

### Keep a separate file for costs

For each claimed business cost, retain the supplier document, payment evidence, purchase date and a short explanation of its connection to the work. If a laptop, internet plan or room also serves personal purposes, record the mixed use and the basis proposed for any allocation. Do not treat the presence of a receipt as automatic proof that the entire amount is deductible.

Give an adviser an organized list of unresolved items: equipment purchases, overseas software subscriptions, payments to editors, gifts received from sponsors, and costs incurred before registration. This is more useful than a folder of unlabelled screenshots.

### Review three separate obligations

1. **Business setup and VAT:** confirm the category and the reporting duties that follow from your actual operation. If staff or facilities change, revisit the earlier classification.
2. **Annual income tax:** use the [NTS income-tax overview](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=7664&mi=2224) to review your income mix. Having no Korean withholding statement is not a substitute for this review.
3. **Cross-border and work permission:** document residence, days worked in Korea, contracts and taxes paid abroad. A tax registration or filed return does not establish permission to carry out an activity under a visa.

The [2026 NTS foreign-individual guide](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?bbsId=30698&mi=10788&nttSn=1350804) concerns returns for **2025**. Check the year printed on any manual before using its examples for another year. The creator page also contains historical examples; do not copy their figures into a current return without checking applicability.

### Leave the preparation stage with a usable file

A useful file contains a revenue map, a payout reconciliation, a cost list, business-registration information and a short list of unanswered classification questions. Once those agree, move to the [income-tax filing procedure](/procedures/how-to-file-income-tax-on-hometax/). If a payer or transaction is still unexplained, resolve it before approving an automatically filled return.
