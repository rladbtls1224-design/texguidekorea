### Check residence history before calculating

NTS guidance says overseas shares sold by a resident who has continuously maintained a Korean domicile or residence for at least five years up to disposal are within the overseas-share capital gains rules. Its published interpretation also addresses a sale before five years of continuous residence.

Build a dated timeline of arrival, residence changes and the proposed sale. A visa expiry date or an entry stamp alone may not resolve tax residence. Do not substitute the separate residence tests used for other foreign-income provisions. Ask for individual advice if departures, a second home or treaty residence make the timeline unclear.

For the broader residence framework, see [Korea tax residency rules](/guides/korea-tax-residency-foreigners/).

### Classify the transaction

| What happened | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| You sold overseas company shares | Overseas-share capital gains and residence history |
| You sold Korean company shares | Domestic-share rules and investor status |
| You received a dividend | Dividend income treatment |
| You vested in or exercised an employment award | Employment benefit first; later sale separately |

Identify the issuer, security, transaction and relevant dates rather than relying only on where the broker is located. Keep fund and ETF documentation when product classification is uncertain.

Use the [Korean stock capital gains guide](/guides/korean-stock-capital-gains-tax-foreigners/) for domestic shares. For employment awards, see [stock options and RSUs](/guides/stock-options-rsu-tax-korea-foreign-employees/).

### Prepare a transaction worksheet

Create one row for each disposal with acquisition references, quantity, sale evidence, costs and fees. Attach records for splits, mergers, transfers or other events affecting the holding. A transferred account can lose the acquisition history displayed by the receiving broker, so obtain the original records early.

| Worksheet item | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- |
| Asset and quantity | Trade confirmation and security identifier |
| Acquisition cost | Original purchase record and relevant adjustments |
| Disposal proceeds | Sale confirmation and settlement record |
| Costs and fees | Brokerage statements and supporting charges |
| KRW calculation | Exchange-rate source, relevant dates and calculation method |

Do not translate a single annual dollar profit into won and assume the calculation is complete. Review the applicable conversion method and transaction dates. See [foreign income conversion to KRW](/guides/convert-foreign-income-krw-korean-tax/) for record preparation.

### Review aggregation and deductions

Before completing a return, check which gains and losses may be combined and which deduction applies. Do not combine unrelated asset classes or claim a deduction independently for every broker account. Use the current NTS overseas-stock materials and retain the computation supporting the result.

A foreign tax payment also needs a separate review. Keep the foreign return or withholding evidence and ask whether relief is available under the applicable domestic rules and treaty. Do not assume the Korean liability is reduced automatically by every amount charged abroad.

### Keep the filing calendar separate

The August 2026 NTS announcement explains that overseas shares do not use the domestic-share preliminary filing route and directs qualifying 2026 gains to the May 2027 final filing period. Check the final deadline and filing requirement for the relevant year, including any applicable adjustment or relief.

Keep capital gains preparation separate from dividend reporting and ordinary annual income tax work. Filing one return does not establish that every other obligation has been completed.

### Before selling or submitting

Confirm residence history, asset classification and available acquisition evidence before a large or complex disposal. Keep copies of the calculation, submitted return and payment confirmation. Also assess overseas financial-account reporting separately; a capital gains return is not a substitute for that review.

This guide was reviewed on October 13, 2026. Recheck the official rules for the disposal year, especially where residence, inherited or gifted holdings, employee awards or cross-border relief affect the result.
