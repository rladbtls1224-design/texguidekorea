### Define the task before naming the representative

Write the tax type, period, form, certificate, office, payment or refund action, and deadline. A representative who is authorized only to request an income certificate should not be assumed able to amend a return or change a refund account.

### Confirm the office's document list

Contact the responsible district tax office or NTS service point and ask which power-of-attorney form, signature or seal, ID copy, translation, notarization, or apostille is required for the specific task. Requirements for a routine certificate can differ from an appeal or audit matter.

### Use the narrowest sufficient authority

State what the representative may submit, receive, sign, correct, pay, and discuss, together with an expiry date. Keep a copy of the signed authority and avoid sending unrelated passport, bank, or authentication data.

### Review before submission

Ask for the completed return, schedules, certificate request, and payment instruction before the representative files. Confirm names, identification numbers, tax periods, bank account, and contact details. A proxy submits for the taxpayer; the taxpayer still needs to understand the result.

### Collect proof of completion

Obtain the filing acceptance, payment receipt, issued certificate, correspondence, and any remaining action list. Revoke or let the authority expire after completion and retain the representation file with the underlying tax records.
