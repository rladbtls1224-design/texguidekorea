### Establish the tax loss

Reconcile revenue and allowable costs first. Loan principal, owner withdrawals and cash spent buying assets do not automatically become current expenses. Keep the books and evidence supporting the calculation.

### Apply the correct restriction

Income Tax Act Article 45 governs losses and carryforwards. It contains distinctions for property rental businesses and different income categories. Do not assume that every loss can offset any salary, dividend or future gain.

### Maintain a year-by-year schedule

Record the originating tax year, original loss, amounts used, remaining balance and applicable expiry. Verify the rules for that loss year, including transitional provisions. Preserve supporting books for the period required to substantiate a carryforward claim, rather than deleting them after an ordinary short retention period.
