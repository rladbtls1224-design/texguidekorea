### Start by separating the payments

A bank deposit described as “parental pay” can represent several different things. Prepare one row for each payment before deciding how to report it.

| Payment | Starting point for review |
| --- | --- |
| Employment Insurance parental-leave or maternity benefit | Confirm the statutory award and payment period |
| Ordinary company salary or leave top-up | Ask payroll for its wage classification and any specific exemption |
| Employer childbirth-support payment | Check birth date, installment history and related-party conditions |
| Employer childcare allowance | Check the applicable monthly ceiling and eligible children |
| Another family or local-government benefit | Identify its own scheme and rules separately |

The [NTS wage-income guide](https://s.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=7867&mi=6431) distinguishes statutory parental-leave benefits from employer family-related payments. A familiar label on a payslip is not enough to choose the correct category.

### Are statutory parental-leave benefits taxable?

[Income Tax Act Article 12(3)(ma)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0012&lsiSeq=280405&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) lists qualifying parental-leave benefits, reduced-hours childcare benefits and maternity benefits received under the Employment Insurance Act as exempt. It also contains provisions for specified public-sector and school arrangements; those should be checked under their own legal basis.

Keep the award notice beside the payment history. Do not assume a company-funded payment is the same statutory benefit simply because both arrive during leave.

Tax classification does not itself establish entitlement to a benefit. Foreign employees should confirm insurance coverage, award conditions and documentation with the administering office.

### When can employer childbirth support be exempt?

The [NTS explanation](https://s.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=7867&mi=6431) describes full exemption for qualifying employer payments connected to the employee's or spouse's childbirth, subject to statutory conditions.

Under [Decree Article 17-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=02&joNo=0017&lsiSeq=290841&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR), the count concerns the **first and second qualifying payments within two years after birth**, and is calculated per employer. The decree also defines excluded related-party relationships: the test differs for an individual employer and a corporate employer.

Make a dated record of each installment. Do not select whichever two payments are largest or assume a third installment has the same treatment. Ask payroll to explain the treatment of any amount outside the qualifying rule.

For example, if your employer pays support in January and July and considers another installment in December, list all three before asking about exemption. The calendar alone does not establish eligibility; the payment purpose, birth date and other conditions still matter.

### What is the 2026 childcare-allowance rule?

This monthly allowance is a separate category from childbirth support. The [government's 2026 changes notice](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148957488) states that, for payments from January 1, 2026, the ceiling changed from KRW 200,000 per worker per month to **KRW 200,000 per eligible child per month**.

[Article 12(3)(meo)(2)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0012&lsiSeq=280405&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) uses the child's age at the beginning of the tax year for the qualifying age test. Ask payroll to check the statutory age definition against the child's exact date of birth rather than relying on an informal age description.

Illustration: if two children both qualify, the combined monthly ceiling is KRW 400,000. If the qualifying allowance actually paid is KRW 300,000, the ceiling does not create another KRW 100,000 of cash entitlement. Do not apply this 2026 ceiling retrospectively to older payments without reviewing that year's rules.

### Does a foreign-worker flat-tax election change the answer?

The [NTS foreign-worker guidance issued in January 2026](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=2201&nttSn=1347723) explains that the special foreign-worker flat-tax treatment excludes ordinary income-tax exemptions, deductions and relief within its scope. Ask payroll to review employer childbirth support and childcare allowances in that comparison.

Do not assume that a special election for employment income changes the classification of every statutory benefit you receive. Identify which payments are employment compensation and obtain a separate explanation for any benefit award. See the [foreign-worker flat-tax guide](/guides/flat-tax-rate-foreign-workers-korea/).

### Can employer payments affect my leave benefit?

Tax exemption and benefit calculation are different questions. [Employment Insurance Act Enforcement Decree Article 98](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?ancYnChk=0&chrClsCd=010202&efYd=20260918&lsiSeq=288717&urlMode=lsInfoP) provides for adjustment where employer payments made because of parental leave, together with the statutory leave benefit, exceed the relevant ordinary-wage amount, subject to its detailed rules.

Keep company top-ups visible when dealing with the benefit office. An employer's “non-taxable” payroll label is not permission to omit the payment from information requested for a benefit claim.

### Build a record that payroll can reconcile

Use a worksheet with these columns:

- Payer and payment description.
- Amount, payment date and covered period.
- Award notice or employer approval reference.
- Proposed taxable or exempt classification.
- Supporting rule and any unresolved question.

Match the worksheet to deposits and payslips without counting a notice and its corresponding deposit as two payments. Keep later corrections linked to the original entry.

Before [year-end settlement](/guides/year-end-tax-settlement-foreigners/), ask payroll to explain differences between the worksheet and the [withholding receipt](/guides/withholding-tax-receipt-korea/). Retain the written response and corrected statement where relevant.

A payment exemption is also distinct from a [dependent deduction](/guides/dependent-deductions-foreign-workers-korea/). Check each claim separately instead of assuming one establishes the other.
