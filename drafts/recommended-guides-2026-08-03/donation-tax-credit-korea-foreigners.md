### Verify the organization before claiming

Request the formal donation receipt and confirm the organization's eligible classification. A transfer confirmation or thank-you message does not by itself establish a Korean tax credit.

### Use the correct category

Political, statutory, employee stock ownership, religious, and other eligible donations may have different rates, ordering, ceilings, and carryforward rules.

### Reconcile current and prior years

Check Hometax data against paper receipts and any unused donations carried forward. Maintain a schedule showing the original donation year, amount used, and remaining balance.

### Coordinate with the employer

Submit evidence by the employer's deadline and retain copies. If the donation was omitted, review whether a later return or correction claim is available.
