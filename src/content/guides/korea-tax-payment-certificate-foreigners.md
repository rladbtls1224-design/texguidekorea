### Confirm the Korean certificate name first

An English request for a "tax payment certificate" can lead to the wrong service. The [NTS English certificate list](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=238885&mi=40036) treats a Certificate of Tax Payment and a Tax Clearance Certificate as separate items. Get the Korean name from the receiving institution before logging in.

| Document | Question it addresses | Do not confuse it with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 납세증명서 — national tax clearance | National tax arrears at issuance, with statutory exceptions | A list of payments or proof of annual income |
| 납부내역증명 — national payment history | National tax payments for the selected period and scope | Current clearance of arrears |
| 지방세 납세증명서 — local tax clearance | Local tax clearance under the separate local service | National tax clearance |
| 소득금액증명 — income certificate | Taxable income amounts for the covered income year | The amount of tax paid |

[Government24's national clearance description](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000011) concerns the absence of national tax arrears at issuance, excluding specified extensions or suspensions and other legal exceptions. It should not be described as a statement of every tax payment you have ever made.

The separate [payment-history service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000018) is the starting point when the request is for 납부내역증명. [Local clearance](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=13100000056) and [income certificates](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000021) have their own service pages.

### Follow an issuance checklist

Use this sequence as preparation; the official service screen controls the available options.

1. **Record the request.** Write down the Korean title, whose certificate is needed, whether it concerns national or local tax, required period, language, and issue-date or validity conditions.
2. **Open the matching official service.** Use the Government24 page linked above and the official application route it provides. Check the domain before entering identity details.
3. **Check who may apply.** Prepare the required identification and authentication. Government24 states that online representative applications are unavailable for national tax clearance. For in-person representation, the NTS lists a power of attorney and identification documents; read the full requirements before visiting.
4. **Choose the correct scope.** For payment history, follow the requested payment period and tax scope. For clearance, check the requested purpose and issue-date conditions. Do not copy an income-year selection from a different certificate request.
5. **Read the issued result.** Compare the name, certificate title, covered scope, language, issue date, and any printed validity date with the receiving institution's instructions.
6. **Keep a complete submission copy.** Retain all pages and verification markings in the required format. Keep the request alongside the result so that another person can see which item it is intended to satisfy.

A successful issuance does not establish that the document is the one the receiving institution accepts. Ask about substitutes before submitting a different certificate.

### Troubleshoot the specific problem

**Clearance is unavailable:** save the exact message and identify the authority responsible for the certificate. If the message concerns arrears, establish which tax and period are involved. If you have already paid, retain the payment reference and ask the authority to confirm the record rather than assuming that a bank transfer alone completed the issue.

**Payment history is empty or unexpected:** compare the requested payment dates, tax type, taxpayer details, and your payment evidence. An income year and the date a tax payment was received are different fields. Ask the issuing office which scope to select when the institution's request is unclear.

**National records look correct but local clearance is still requested:** obtain the local document through its own service. A national document does not become local clearance because it refers to the same taxpayer.

**The institution wants an income amount:** return to the [document-selection guide](/guides/tax-documents-visa-extension-korea/). A tax payment figure is not a substitute for the annual income amount the institution has asked to assess.

For a packet with wrong names, missing income, release-date questions, or rejected printouts, use the [visa document troubleshooting checklist](/guides/korean-tax-documents-visa-extension/). Keep the tax authority's explanation of its records separate from the receiving institution's decision on document acceptance.
