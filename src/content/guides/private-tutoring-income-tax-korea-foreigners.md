### Classify the actual relationship

The contract label is only a starting point. A tutor directed by one academy under its schedule and supervision may resemble an employee. A person independently offering recurring lessons to multiple students may have business income. A genuinely isolated lecture can fall into other income. Frequency, independence, control, and the commercial setup matter together.

### Understand what 3.3% means

When a payer withholds 3% national income tax plus local income tax from qualifying business-service payments, the deduction is usually a prepayment. It is not proof that the classification is correct and it is not automatically the final liability. The tutor normally reconciles annual income, expenses, deductions, and prepaid tax through the applicable May filing.

### Check permission outside the tax system

Immigration permission, private-tutoring rules, business registration, and tax reporting answer different legal questions. Paying tax does not authorize work that a visa prohibits. Before advertising or accepting students, confirm permitted activities with immigration and ask the competent education or local office whether a tutoring report or registration is required.

### Keep a tutoring ledger

Record lesson date, student or payer, gross fee, refund, platform fee, withholding, and net receipt. Store agreements, schedules, and payment evidence. For expenses, preserve the business purpose and separate personal portions. A home, phone, computer, transport, or training cost is not fully deductible merely because tutoring occurs.

### Reconcile before filing

Compare your ledger with Hometax payer submissions and withholding statements. Investigate missing or duplicated amounts. Report foreign-platform or overseas-client payments based on the applicable residence and source rules rather than assuming they disappear because the payer is abroad.
