### Separate the tax and period

Read the tax name before making a payment. November income tax interim prepayment is not October VAT. Identify the taxpayer, calculation basis and instructions on the current notice.

### Review the first-half reporting route

A lower current-year profit does not automatically cancel a notice. The NTS interim-prepayment guidance explains estimated reporting using first-half results and the conditions for that route. Some bookkeeping taxpayers without a prior payment base may also have a reporting obligation. Check the applicable category before relying on the absence of a notice.

### Reconcile at annual filing

Retain the payment confirmation and calculation papers for the following annual return. Interim prepayment is part of the income tax settlement process; it is not evidence that all annual income has already been reported. Confirm current deadlines and any extension in the latest NTS guidance.

Confirm the rules and forms for the relevant tax year using the official sources below. This guide is general information; complex residence, classification or disputed-assessment questions need individual review.
