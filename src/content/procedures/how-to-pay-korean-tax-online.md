### Make one row for each obligation

Before paying, copy the details from the return or official notice into a short list. Keep national and local items on separate rows even when they relate to the same income year.

| Field | Why you check it |
| --- | --- |
| Taxpayer and identifier | To ensure you are paying the intended person's liability |
| Tax type and authority | To choose national or local payment correctly |
| Income year or tax period | To avoid paying an older or different item |
| Payment reference | To match the transaction to the correct liability |
| Amount and due date | To compare the current notice with your filing records |
| Existing payment or approved extension | To avoid duplication or relying on an assumed deadline |

If an accountant filed for you, obtain the filing receipt and payment instructions. A statement that “the return is done” does not tell you whether each payment was completed.

### Follow the current official route

The [NTS income-tax payment instructions](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=238910&mi=40296) give this national-tax path:

**Hometax login → 납부·고지·환급 → 세금납부 → 납부할세액 조회/납부 → 납부하기**

These labels lead from payment/notice/refund to tax payment, amount-due enquiry/payment, and payment. The page specifies joint or financial certificate authentication for this route and lists bank transfer, card and simplified payment options. Follow the requirements actually shown for your transaction.

The same NTS page states that **CardroTax ended service from May 22, 2026**. An older guide may still name that service; use the current NTS directions instead. The separate [national electronic-payment guidance](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=7741&mi=2364) also identifies Hometax and Internet Giro as official routes.

For personal local income tax, use the local notice or the relevant official local service. The [2026 Seoul guidance](https://www.seoul.go.kr/news/news_report.do?nttNo=458228) describes Wetax and Seoul ETAX routes. Do not reuse a national payment reference for a local bill.

### Check before authorizing

Read the final screen against your list: taxpayer, tax type, period, reference and amount. Review any payment charge or condition displayed before approving the transaction. If the amount differs from the expected balance, save the explanation or ask the authority instead of overwriting a figure simply to make it match your worksheet.

Use a reference or virtual account taken from an authenticated official record or your genuine notice. When a message contains a payment link, open the official service independently and locate the obligation there before sending money.

### Distinguish three documents

A filing receipt records a return submission. A payment slip describes how to pay. A completed-payment receipt records the payment result. Retain all applicable documents and connect them with the same tax reference.

After payment, compare the official completed result with your bank or card record. Note the payment date, amount and confirmation reference. If a receiving institution later asks for a tax certificate, confirm which certificate it requires; do not assume a payment screenshot is a substitute.

### Fictional example: two slips, one completed payment

Taylor has a national income-tax slip and a local income-tax slip for the same income year. The national payment succeeds. Taylor saves that receipt but leaves the local row unresolved until its own payment result is confirmed. This avoids treating one successful transaction as evidence for both obligations.

### If a payment window stops responding

Do not immediately repeat the payment. Record the time, reference and displayed error, then check the official payment result and the bank transaction. If one shows a debit and the other does not show completion, ask the payment service or authority to reconcile them. Keep the evidence while the issue is investigated.

For deadlines, use the actual notice and any approved extension. A general article cannot establish that your filing deadline and payment deadline were both extended. Once every applicable row has matching completed-payment evidence, retain the file with your [income-tax return](/procedures/how-to-file-income-tax-on-hometax/).
