### October 2026: two different deadlines

The NTS announcement dated October 5, 2026 distinguishes these dates:

| Action | Announced deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminary VAT return and associated payment | October 26, 2026 |
| Preliminary notice payment | November 2, 2026 |

These are cycle-specific dates, not a permanent annual calendar. Check your own notice and any applicable relief separately.

### Identify what arrived

Look for the tax name, business registration number, period, amount and payment reference. Keep the complete document and record its delivery date. A notice can be a routine advance payment; receiving one does not by itself establish that you failed to file.

Do not confuse VAT with a global income tax or local income tax bill. If the document belongs to another business, or the registration number is wrong, resolve that mismatch before making a payment.

### Notice payment versus preliminary return

| Document or route | Practical meaning | Your first check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary notice / 예정고지 | Pay an amount assessed in advance | Match the notice to your registration and prior VAT records |
| Preliminary return / 예정신고 | Submit a return for the relevant interim period | Confirm your taxpayer type and filing obligation |
| Final return / 확정신고 | Settle the taxable period | Reconcile prior payments against the final calculation |

The NTS VAT overview describes advance notices for individual general taxpayers and qualifying small corporations, generally based on half the prior six-month period's VAT liability. Simplified taxpayers follow a different framework. Foreign ownership alone does not identify the VAT category.

### If sales fell or you expect an early refund

Do not ignore the notice or replace it with a payment of your own choosing. NTS guidance provides an alternative preliminary-return route in qualifying circumstances involving business deterioration or early refunds. Confirm eligibility, the required period and the submission deadline with current official instructions or your tax representative. Retain proof that any alternative procedure was accepted.

Prepare comparable sales and purchase records rather than relying only on a bank balance. Document which invoices belong to the period and investigate missing entries before asking an adviser to assess your route.

### Check the amount online and pay carefully

Sign in to the official [Hometax website](https://www.hometax.go.kr/). The October announcement lists the preliminary-notice lookup under VAT filing and filing-help data. Menu labels may change; search for 예정고지 세액 조회 if needed.

Compare the online amount with the paper or electronic notice. Use official payment instructions and confirm the business identity, tax period and payment reference. Save the confirmation after payment rather than treating an opened payment screen as proof of completion.

For payment navigation, see [how to pay Korean tax online](/procedures/how-to-pay-korean-tax-online/). For the broader filing calendar, see [Korea VAT filing deadlines](/guides/korea-vat-filing-deadlines-foreign-business/).

### Keep the final-return credit trail

The NTS explains that preliminary notice tax is credited in the final VAT settlement. Store the notice, payment confirmation and relevant return together, and reconcile the prepaid amount when filing. Investigate discrepancies before submitting rather than assuming every figure was imported correctly.

A useful folder contains the notice, prior final return, current ledger, invoice evidence, payment confirmation and any accepted alternative filing. Name files by business and period so your adviser can trace the calculation.

### Before the deadline

Check the current announcement again if your business may qualify for special relief. Confirm the actual status of any extension or alternative filing instead of assuming that another business's treatment applies. Ask the tax office or a qualified representative when the taxpayer category, amount or procedure remains unclear.
