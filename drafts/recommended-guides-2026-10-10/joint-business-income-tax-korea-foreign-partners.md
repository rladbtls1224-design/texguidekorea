### Identify the legal structure

Separate a jointly operated individual business from a corporation, an ordinary service contract or a passive investment. The partners' foreign nationality does not by itself choose the structure or determine its tax treatment.

### Calculate the business result

Article 43 establishes a joint-business-level income calculation. Reconcile the business's revenue and costs before preparing partner information. Money withdrawn by a partner is not automatically a wage expense or a complete measure of that person's taxable share.

### Document the allocation

Retain the agreement, ownership evidence and calculation supporting each partner's share. Review the applicable allocation rules, changes during the year, related-party arrangements and passive-investor distinctions under current law. Each partner should separately check residence and annual filing obligations. Confirm registration and immigration requirements before beginning operations.

### Before you submit

Confirm the rules and forms for the relevant tax year using the official sources below. This guide is general information; complex residence, classification or disputed-assessment questions need individual review.
