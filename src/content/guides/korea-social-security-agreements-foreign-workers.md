### Identify the agreement and worker category

Use the NPS country guide for the specific country. Confirm the effective dates, covered programs, detached-worker period, and rules for employees, self-employed people, crew, civil servants, or dual activity. Do not import a five-year assignment rule from a different agreement.

### Obtain the correct certificate

A worker coming to Korea generally needs evidence from the other country's competent authority; a worker remaining under Korean coverage while abroad generally requests a Korean certificate through NPS. Coordinate the certificate before payroll enrollment where possible and keep proof that the Korean employer or NPS received it.

### Separate exemption from totalization

An exemption prevents duplicate contributions for a qualifying period. Totalization can combine periods solely to test benefit eligibility, while each country normally calculates and pays its own portion under its rules. Neither function automatically produces a cash refund.

### Reconcile payroll and contribution history

Compare payroll deductions with the certificate period and NPS record. If contributions continued after an exemption should have applied, ask about correction and refund procedures promptly. Review income tax and other social insurance lines separately so one approved exemption is not extended beyond its legal scope.
