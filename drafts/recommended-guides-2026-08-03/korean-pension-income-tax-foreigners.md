### Name the pension source

Separate National Pension, occupational retirement benefits, pension savings, IRP, foreign pension, and ordinary investment withdrawals. Request the plan's Korean classification.

### Map contributions to benefits

Record who contributed, which contributions received deductions or credits, and whether employer retirement money entered the account. This history can affect later taxation.

### Test residence and treaty rules

Determine tax residence when the payment is made and read the relevant treaty article. Social security pensions, private pensions, government service, and lump sums may not share one rule.

### Reconcile withholding

Keep the gross payment, tax withheld, exchange-rate evidence, and foreign-country reporting. If tax was withheld inconsistently with a treaty, review the refund or correction procedure promptly.
