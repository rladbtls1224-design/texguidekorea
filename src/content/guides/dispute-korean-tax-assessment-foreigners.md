### Identify the document before selecting a remedy

Keep the original Korean notice and all attachments. Note the issuing authority, tax type, period, reference number and amount. A translation can help you understand the issue, but keep the Korean document available when asking which procedure applies.

This guide focuses on national tax procedures explained by the NTS. Do not automatically apply the same form or authority to a local-tax notice. For an unfamiliar letter, start with [Korean tax notices and letters](/guides/korean-tax-notices-letters-foreigners/).

### Separate the stages

| What you received | Route to investigate | First evidence to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifying proposed assessment or audit-results notice | Pre-assessment review / 과세전적부심사 | Original notice and receipt date |
| Issued national tax assessment | Objection or another applicable post-assessment remedy | Assessment, service evidence and relevant decision |
| An error you found in your own return | Correction or refund-claim procedure | Filed return and corrected calculation |

NTS explains that pre-assessment review occurs before a tax assessment is issued. Its guidance describes a 30-day filing window following receipt of a qualifying notice. Check eligibility and the exact notice rather than assuming that every tax letter opens this route.

Post-assessment options include objection, review and tribunal procedures. NTS describes a 90-day period linked to knowledge or notification of the relevant disposition. Confirm the correct route and trigger for your case; do not treat all available remedies as compulsory consecutive steps.

### Record the deadline evidence

Save the envelope, postal tracking, electronic delivery record and any acknowledgement. Write a short timeline showing receipt, enquiries, submissions and responses. If dates conflict, flag that issue for your representative immediately.

Ask which document starts the applicable clock and how submission must be completed. An appointment booking or an informal exchange is not a substitute for checking the formal filing requirements. If evidence is incomplete, ask promptly how to preserve the claim and supply supporting material through the permitted procedure.

### Build a focused case file

For each disputed item, describe what the tax office concluded, what correction you seek and which evidence supports it. Avoid sending an unexplained folder of bank statements.

| Case-file section | Useful contents |
| --- | --- |
| Decision being challenged | Notice reference, period and disputed amount |
| Facts | Dated account of the transaction or income |
| Calculation | Tax-office figure alongside your proposed correction |
| Evidence | Numbered contracts, invoices, statements or residence records |
| Requested outcome | Specific item or amount you ask the authority to reconsider |

Keep originals and a copy of the exact submission. Ask a qualified representative to assess legal arguments, procedure choices and any translation or representation requirements. For an audit-related dispute, see [tax audit preparation](/guides/korean-tax-audit-preparation-foreigners/).

### Separate the dispute from payment questions

Record the payment deadline even if you challenge the assessment. Confirm the collection position and any separate application required for relief. Do not infer a suspension solely from an appeal receipt.

If the issue instead concerns your own filing, review [amending a Korean return and claiming a refund](/guides/amend-korean-tax-return-refund-claim/) before selecting a dispute form.

### Track submission and the response

Retain the official submission receipt, attachment list and correspondence. Check for requests to supplement the file. On receiving a decision, preserve its delivery evidence and confirm any next-step deadline promptly.

This guide was reviewed on October 18, 2026. It is an orientation to official national-tax procedures, not a calculation of your individual appeal deadline. Obtain case-specific assistance where service dates, jurisdiction or available remedies are unclear.
