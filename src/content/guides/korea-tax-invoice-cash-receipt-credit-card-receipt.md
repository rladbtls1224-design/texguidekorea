### Match the document to the transaction

Before paying, identify whether the supplier is VAT-registered, whether the supply is taxable or exempt, whether the buyer is acting as a business, and how payment will be made. Ask the supplier which qualifying document will be issued and provide the buyer's registered details accurately.

### Understand the three evidence paths

An electronic tax invoice reports the supplier, buyer, supply value, VAT, and supply date. An expense-proof cash receipt can identify a business purchaser when cash or a covered cash-like method is used. An eligible business card slip records a card purchase and can support the prescribed tax treatment. Each document should be reconciled to the underlying supply rather than collected mechanically.

### Avoid duplicate reporting

One purchase should appear once in the expense and VAT ledgers. When a payment method already produces qualifying evidence, confirm whether a separate tax invoice is permitted or necessary. Match cancellations, partial refunds, and corrected documents so the original amount is not left in the return.

### Check Hometax and retain context

Download or review the available purchase data, then match it to contracts, delivery, payment, and business purpose. Hometax data helps reconciliation but does not prove that every item is deductible or business-related.
