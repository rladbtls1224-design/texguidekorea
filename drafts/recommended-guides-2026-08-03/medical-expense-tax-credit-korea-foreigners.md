### Check the tax method first

Ordinary income deductions and credits are generally relevant when the employee uses progressive taxation. A worker choosing the special foreign-worker flat tax should compare the full result because that method normally replaces ordinary deductions and credits.

### Match the patient and payment

Create a list showing the patient, relationship, provider, amount paid, reimbursement, and whether the item appears in the simplified service. Rules can differ for the employee, older or disabled family members, and other eligible dependents.

### Reconcile Hometax with receipts

Simplified-service data is a starting point, not proof that every displayed item is claimable or that every eligible item is displayed. Obtain receipts for missing eligible expenses and remove reimbursements or excluded items.

### Preserve the calculation

Keep the employer submission, receipts, reimbursement evidence, and a short worksheet explaining the amount claimed. This helps if the employer requests clarification or the deduction is reviewed later.
