### What is Korea's SME employee tax reduction?

The scheme called **중소기업 취업자 소득세 감면** concerns eligible wages paid by a qualifying small or medium-sized enterprise. It is distinct from a company's own business-tax relief and from the special [foreign-engineer reduction](/guides/foreign-engineer-tax-reduction-korea-2026/).

A useful starting question for payroll is: “Can you check my eligibility under Article 30, including the employer's principal industry and my previous qualifying employment?”

### What does the current law provide?

[Article 30](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028573657), reviewed for this guide on October 25, 2026, provides the following framework:

| Qualifying category | Reduction rate | Ordinary period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Young worker | 90% | Five years |
| Age 60 or over, qualifying disability, or qualifying career-interrupted worker | 70% | Three years |

The cap is **KRW 2 million per tax year**. The law currently specifies qualifying entry into employment through **December 31, 2026**. That entry-date limit is distinct from the end of an individual's reduction period. Special military-return provisions can affect the timeline.

Changing employers does not ordinarily restart the original reduction clock. These points come from Article 30; proposed extensions should not be treated as enacted rules.

### Can foreign workers qualify?

Our reading of the cited law is that nationality alone is not a general exclusion: [Article 30](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028573657) and [Decree Article 27](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0027&lsiSeq=288915&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) define qualifying categories and exclusions without a blanket Korean-citizenship condition. This is a statutory reading, not approval of an individual claim.

Under Article 27, the young-worker category generally uses age 15–34 at contract signing; the older-worker category uses age 60 or above. A military-service age adjustment applies only to the service specified in the rule. Do not assume overseas service qualifies.

The exclusions include specified executives, controlling owners and related persons, daily workers, and certain cases where required pension or workplace health-insurance payment cannot be confirmed, subject to stated exceptions. Ask payroll to document how any exception applies.

For career interruption, use the current statutory cross-reference rather than assuming every return to work qualifies. Some NTS overview text still uses the older “career-interrupted women” description; the current Act and Decree refer to qualifying career-interrupted workers.

### Is my employer eligible?

The [NTS overview](https://t.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=239023&mi=40611) requires both SME status and an eligible principal business activity. A workplace's headcount or use of the word “startup” is insufficient.

Ask HR for a written response to these checks:

| Question | Record to retain |
| --- | --- |
| Is the employer an SME for this scheme? | Employer's eligibility explanation |
| What is its principal industry? | Industry classification and supporting business records |
| Is that activity covered by Article 27? | Applicable industry provision |
| Does an exclusion apply? | Explanation for any ownership, role or insurance issue |

Article 27 lists eligible industries with exceptions. For example, it separately lists technical/vocational training and computer academies. Do not infer that every language school or teaching job qualifies because it is an educational business.

### How do I apply through payroll?

The [NTS process overview](https://t.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=239023&mi=40611) distinguishes the employee's application from the employer's submission to the tax office.

1. Gather your contract, category evidence and earlier reduction records.
2. Ask payroll to verify the employer and employee conditions.
3. Complete the **중소기업 취업자 소득세 감면신청서** using the applicable form.
4. Keep a dated copy and obtain confirmation of receipt.
5. Check later payslips and the annual withholding receipt for how the reduction was handled.

The ordinary employee submission timing under Article 27 is the end of the month following the employment month. Article 30 requires the employer's beneficiary list by the tenth day of the month after it receives the application. A retired worker can apply to the tax office with jurisdiction over their address under Article 30.

If you missed a step, ask about the appropriate correction procedure for that year. Do not enter an unsupported claim simply to make a forecast show a refund.

### How much would I actually save?

A reduction percentage is not a percentage of salary. It also does not guarantee that much cash back. Eligible wage amounts, other income, the annual cap and interactions in the tax calculation matter.

For illustration only, suppose payroll has calculated KRW 1 million as the tax amount to which the applicable reduction percentage is applied. At 90%, the preliminary reduction is KRW 900,000. If that base were KRW 3 million, 90% would be KRW 2.7 million before applying the annual cap. These examples do not calculate anyone's final tax or refund.

Keep a worksheet showing payroll's eligible base, applied rate, cap and resulting annual liability. Ask how the reduction affects other items in the calculation, rather than subtracting it mechanically from take-home pay. Local income tax and social insurance should remain identifiable.

### Can I combine it with the foreign-worker flat tax?

The [NTS foreign-worker guidance issued in January 2026](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=2201&nttSn=1347723) explains that electing the foreign-worker flat-tax treatment excludes the ordinary exemptions, deductions and reductions covered by that restriction. Do not stack this SME reduction on top of the special flat-tax calculation.

Ask for two complete comparisons using consistent compensation figures: ordinary taxation with eligible relief, and special flat-tax treatment if available. A lower withholding amount on one payslip does not settle which annual outcome is better. See the [foreign-worker flat-tax guide](/guides/flat-tax-rate-foreign-workers-korea/).

### What should I check before year-end settlement?

Create a short record of unresolved questions: employer eligibility, original start date, remaining period, missing evidence or a past-year omission. Have payroll resolve those items before you rely on an estimated refund.

Use your [withholding receipt](/guides/withholding-tax-receipt-korea/) to reconcile the outcome. If you changed employers, keep the [job-change checklist](/guides/job-change-year-end-settlement-korea-foreigners/) beside the application. For an earlier-year omission, review [correction and refund-claim options](/guides/amend-korean-tax-return-refund-claim/) with the tax office or representative.
