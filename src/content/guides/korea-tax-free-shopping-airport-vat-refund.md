### Choose the correct refund route

At an immediate-refund store, eligible tax is deducted during purchase after passport verification. Under the general system, pay the tax-inclusive price and receive a separate refund voucher for a later downtown, mobile, airport, port, or mailbox process. Follow the operator and departure point shown on the voucher.

### Protect the evidence and goods

Check that the passport name and receipt details are correct before leaving the store. Keep the goods unused and available, retain the voucher and original receipt, and do not place inspection-required goods into checked baggage before customs validation.

### Complete departure validation in order

Allow extra time. At the airport or port, obtain the boarding pass as instructed, scan the passport and vouchers, complete customs inspection when prompted, then check baggage and collect or trigger the refund at the designated counter or kiosk. Terminal procedures can differ.

### Troubleshoot a missing refund

Identify the refund operator on the voucher and keep the transaction number. Confirm that departure validation completed, the card details were accepted, and processing time has passed. Tourist VAT refunds are separate from Korean income-tax refunds and are not tracked through an ordinary Hometax income-tax return.
