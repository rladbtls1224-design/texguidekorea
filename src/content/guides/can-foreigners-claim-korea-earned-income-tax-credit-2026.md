### Check the nationality exception first

Start with the applicant's status on December 31, 2025. The NTS states that a person who did not hold Korean nationality on that date is generally excluded. It then provides exceptions for a person married to a Korean national and a person with a Korean-national dependent child. A long visa, permanent-resident status, or many years in Korea does not replace this specific nationality test.

### Build the correct household

The credit is household-based. Identify the applicant, spouse, dependent children, and relevant direct ascendants or descendants under the NTS household definitions. Then classify the household as single, single-income, or dual-income. A family member's address, age, relationship, and income can change the classification and the benefit calculation.

### Reconcile every income source

For a 2026 claim based on 2025 facts, collect both spouses' wage income and any business, religious, interest, dividend, pension, or other income included by the official rules. Do not use only take-home pay. Business receipts may be adjusted under industry ratios, and some types of income are excluded from the payment calculation even though they still matter during the eligibility review.

### Prepare the household property schedule

List housing, land, buildings, lease deposits, financial assets, securities, vehicles, memberships, and rights to acquire real estate held by household members on the relevant reference date. The NTS specifically warns that liabilities are not deducted from the property total in the ordinary way. Lease deposits can also be valued under statutory assumptions rather than only the cash amount written in a contract.

### Choose the correct application route

The regular and half-year routes are not interchangeable. The half-year system is designed for qualifying households with wage income only and is later reconciled against annual facts. If either spouse has business income or another disqualifying income type, verify whether the household must use the regular application. Apply through the current Hometax path or a tax office, keep the confirmation, and monitor requests for corrected family, income, or property evidence.
