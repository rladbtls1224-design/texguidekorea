### Start with the F-5 subtype

F-5 is a family of permanent-residence categories, not a single checklist. Income thresholds, evaluation periods, exemptions, and whether family income can be counted may differ. Download the current checklist for the exact subtype and confirm uncertain points with immigration before relying on an older blog or another applicant's case.

### Separate four kinds of evidence

Use a document matrix:

| Evidence | What it generally shows |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of Income Amount | Income reported to and recorded by the NTS for a stated year |
| Tax-payment certificate | Whether covered national taxes are outstanding as of issuance |
| Local tax certificate | Local-tax payment status for the requested scope |
| Contract, payslips, and bank records | Current pay and cash flow, subject to immigration acceptance |

One document cannot safely stand in for all four questions.

### Check the timing gap

An applicant may have a higher current salary than the income shown for the last completed tax year. A new freelancer may also have bank deposits before an annual return is filed. Obtain certificates early, identify the period they cover, and ask what supplementary proof the immigration office accepts. Do not relabel unreported deposits as certified income.

### Reconcile before submission

Compare the certificate with withholding receipts, filed returns, employer details, and business records. If income is missing, find out whether the payer failed to submit data, the filing year is not yet available, or a return needs correction. If tax is unpaid, resolve or formally clarify it before requesting a fresh certificate.

### Build a reviewer-friendly packet

Place the official subtype checklist first, followed by the income certificate, tax-payment certificates, and only then explanatory records. Add a one-page timeline for employer changes, business income, parental leave, or other facts that create a visible mismatch. Use consistent names, registration numbers, tax years, and translations throughout.
