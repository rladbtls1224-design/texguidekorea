### Identify the procedural stage

A proposed assessment and an issued tax assessment use different review routes. The NTS distinguishes pre-assessment review from remedies available after assessment. Do not choose a form based only on an English translation of the letter.

### Preserve the deadline evidence

Record when and how the document was received. Keep the full notice, attachments and delivery evidence. Confirm the applicable deadline immediately; do not assume that a phone enquiry or an informal complaint preserves appeal rights.

### Prepare a focused case file

Describe the disputed item, the tax office's reasoning, your requested correction and the evidence supporting it. Review the available route with a qualified representative when needed. A refund correction request, an appeal against an assessment and a payment arrangement address different problems. Confirm collection and payment consequences separately.

### Before you submit

Confirm the rules and forms for the relevant tax year using the official sources below. This guide is general information; complex residence, classification or disputed-assessment questions need individual review.
