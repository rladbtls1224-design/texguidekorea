### Check the actual exemption

Confirm whether the activity is VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated or merely operated by a simplified taxpayer. These classifications have different compliance consequences.

### Review the annual report

NTS describes business status reporting for VAT-exempt individual businesses, including certain businesses closed or suspended during the reporting year. Check exclusions, industry schedules and required statement summaries before assuming the rule applies identically to every exempt operator.

### Reconcile revenue once

Match annual receipts to the ledger and supporting statements. Save the submitted report for the later income tax return. Business status reporting and annual income tax filing serve different purposes; completing one does not automatically complete the other. Verify the current reporting window and any special industry requirements before submission.

### Before you submit

Confirm the rules and forms for the relevant tax year using the official sources below. This guide is general information; complex residence, classification or disputed-assessment questions need individual review.
