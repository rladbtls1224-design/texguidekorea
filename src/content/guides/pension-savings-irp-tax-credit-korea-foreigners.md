### Identify the account

Confirm whether the product is pension savings, an IRP, an employer retirement plan, National Pension, or an ordinary investment account. Similar retirement language does not mean identical tax treatment.

### Check current limits

Use the NTS guide for the relevant tax year to verify qualifying contributions, combined ceilings, and credit rates. Do not copy a prior-year limit into a current estimate.

### Compare the lifetime result

Model both the year-end credit and the tax treatment of future pension payments or early withdrawals. Include product fees, investment options, residence plans, and the possibility of leaving Korea.

### Reconcile the certificate

Match the financial institution's contribution certificate to Hometax and the employer submission. Resolve returned contributions or late deposits before claiming the credit.
