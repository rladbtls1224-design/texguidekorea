### Check residence history before calculating

Do not apply a universal overseas-share tax answer to every foreign resident. The NTS has published an interpretation addressing overseas shares sold before five years of continuous Korean residence. Confirm the facts and current rule for your own transaction rather than confusing that test with other foreign-income rules.

### Identify the asset

Separate overseas shares from Korean-listed securities, dividends and employment income arising from stock awards. An investment labelled an ETF by a broker still needs a classification check. A later sale and an earlier employment benefit are separate events.

### Build a Korean reporting worksheet

Collect each acquisition and disposal, fees, corporate actions and supporting exchange-rate records. Review any permitted loss aggregation, deduction and filing timetable in the current NTS overseas-stock guide. A foreign broker's profit display is not a substitute for a Korean tax computation.

### Before you submit

Confirm the rules and forms for the relevant tax year using the official sources below. This guide is general information; complex residence, classification or disputed-assessment questions need individual review.
