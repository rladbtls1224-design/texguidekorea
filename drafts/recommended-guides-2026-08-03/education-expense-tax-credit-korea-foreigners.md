### Separate the student categories

List expenses for the employee, pre-school children, school-age children, university students, and any disabled family member separately. The eligible expense types and ceilings are not identical.

### Verify the institution and fee

Tuition, admission fees, school meals, textbooks, uniforms, after-school programs, and private lessons can receive different treatment. Use the current NTS guide for the tax year rather than relying on the receipt label.

### Review overseas education carefully

An overseas school invoice alone is not enough. Confirm the employee's Korean residence and work facts, the student's eligibility, the institution, and any study-abroad requirements.

### Coordinate family claims

Only one eligible taxpayer should claim the same payment. Keep proof of who paid, the relationship documents, and the employer submission.
