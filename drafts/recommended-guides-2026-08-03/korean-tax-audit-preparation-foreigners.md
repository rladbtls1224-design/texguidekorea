### Verify the document

Confirm the sender, tax office, officer, reference number, tax type, period, issue under review, requested materials, and response date using official contact channels.

### Freeze and inventory records

Preserve paper and electronic records, including email, messaging, cloud files, accounting exports, platform statements, and foreign records. Do not silently reconstruct or overwrite source data.

### Reconcile before responding

Tie filed revenue, withholding, expenses, VAT, assets, overseas income, and bank movements to source documents. List known differences and missing evidence separately.

### Manage communication

Keep a log of calls, submissions, deadlines, and questions. Provide indexed files and concise explanations, request written clarification when scope is unclear, and seek qualified representation when risk is significant.
