### Diagnose the direction of the error

Compare the accepted original return with a corrected computation. Separate missing income, duplicated income, withholding, deductions, credits, expenses, and payment errors.

### Choose the legal route

An amendment that increases tax is different from a claim that reduces tax. A late original return is different again. Confirm the correct procedure and deadline before changing data in Hometax.

### Prepare an audit trail

Create a one-page reconciliation from the original figures to the corrected figures and attach source documents. Explain foreign-currency conversions, income classification, and family eligibility where relevant.

### Track both taxes

Monitor the NTS decision, refund account, and any separate local income tax correction. Keep submission receipts and notices until the process is complete.
