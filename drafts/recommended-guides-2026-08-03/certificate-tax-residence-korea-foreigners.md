### Confirm which certificate is required

Ask the foreign bank, employer, payer, or tax authority whether it needs proof of Korean tax residence, income, tax paid, or tax clearance. These are different documents.

### Define the request precisely

Record the tax year or period, destination country, treaty purpose, and any foreign form that must be signed. A broad request without this information can delay review.

### Apply through the available channel

Check Hometax or contact the competent tax office for the current route. The NTS also lists English certificate forms and identifies Certificate of Residence requests as subject to review.

### Check the issued document

Confirm your romanized name, identification number, covered period, country, and official authentication. Keep the application and the final certificate together.
