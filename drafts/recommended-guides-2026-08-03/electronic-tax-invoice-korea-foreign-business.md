### Identify the document required

Before issuing anything, determine whether the transaction requires a VAT tax invoice, card slip, cash receipt, invoice for an exempt supply, or another document.

### Capture the supply date

Use contracts, delivery, service completion, progress billing, and advance payments to determine the correct issue timing. Commercial payment terms do not always decide the tax date.

### Validate both parties

Check legal name, business registration number, email, value of supply, VAT, and preparation date. Retain the contract and performance evidence behind the invoice.

### Correct through the formal route

If price, buyer, supply, cancellation, or return information changes, use the correct amendment reason and dates in Hometax. Keep the original and correction trail together.
