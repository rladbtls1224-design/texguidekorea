### Identify the business structure first

Keep the business registration beside the agreement. A shared trading name, shared bank account or two people working together does not by itself establish which tax rules apply. Separate a jointly operated individual business from a corporation, employment arrangement or investment contract.

Foreign nationality alone does not determine the answer. If a participant is a company, lives outside Korea or contributes capital without managing the activity, confirm the relevant treatment before using a simple partner allocation.

### Calculate one business result

Income Tax Act Article 43 treats each joint business establishment as one resident for calculating its income. It then allocates income distributed or to be distributed under the agreed profit-and-loss ratio; without an agreed ratio, the ownership ratio applies. This calculation rule does not turn the business into a corporation.

Prepare one reconciled set of annual accounts before passing figures to the partners. For the records workflow, see [bookkeeping requirements](/guides/bookkeeping-requirements-korea-foreign-freelancers/).

A practical preparation sequence is:

1. Match revenue entries to invoices, platform statements and bank receipts.
2. Reconcile expense records and flag items needing a tax adjustment.
3. Keep capital contributions, partner transfers and business trading receipts identifiable in the ledger.
4. Record the final business income figure and the version of the accounts used.
5. Give every partner the same allocation worksheet and retain the supporting agreement.

Do not use a partner's bank withdrawals as the only allocation record. Keep cash movements and the tax-income calculation in separate columns so differences can be explained.

### Keep the allocation evidence together

| Record | What to reconcile |
| --- | --- |
| Joint-business agreement | Participants, allocation terms and effective dates |
| Registration and ownership records | Names and interests against the agreement |
| Annual accounts | The shared business result used in the worksheet |
| Partner allocation worksheet | Each share and a total matching the business figure |
| Cash distribution ledger | Amounts paid, dates and any outstanding balance |

If the agreement changes during the year, preserve the signed versions and effective dates. Ask how the change affects the calculation before applying one ratio to the entire year. Do not invent a retrospective ratio just to match withdrawals.

Article 43 also contains a special rule for specified related-party arrangements involving false allocation ratios or other prescribed circumstances. Family participation is a reason to check the facts and supporting terms, rather than assume an automatic income split. Passive capital-only participants also require separate classification review.

### Review each partner's personal return

NTS guidance describes annual comprehensive income tax reporting for individuals and the need for supporting accounts. Each partner should check their own residence, other income, deductions and applicable filing requirements. Equal business shares do not establish equal final tax bills.

Use a personal checklist alongside the shared worksheet:

- Confirm the taxpayer identity and tax residence information for the relevant year.
- Match the allocated figure to the final business accounts.
- Collect records for other income without duplicating business receipts.
- Record questions about losses, passive investment, related parties or cross-border facts for the tax representative.
- Retain the submitted return and acceptance receipt with the allocation evidence.

The ordinary annual filing period described by NTS is May 1 to May 31 of the following year, with separate rules for qualifying confirmed-filing cases and applicable extensions. Confirm the calendar and instructions for the actual filing year. See the [global income tax filing guide](/guides/global-income-tax-filing-korea-foreigners/) for the broader process.

### Before starting or changing the arrangement

Confirm [business registration requirements](/guides/business-registration-foreign-freelancers-korea/) and any separate work-authorization question. An allocation worksheet does not establish immigration permission or replace registration updates.

If the partners' worksheets disagree, resolve the shared business total and agreement evidence first. Keep a written explanation of the correction and use the same revised source figures when reviewing affected returns.
